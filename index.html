Building learning systems

I am an undergraduate student in Automation at Jilin University. My research interests center on embodied intelligence, especially how vision-language-action policies can be trained, adapted, and evaluated more effectively.

I enjoy work that connects a concrete empirical question with a mechanism that can be tested directly: what a policy has learned, why a training intervention works, and when the same idea stops working.
